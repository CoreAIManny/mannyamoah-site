If you run a care agency, this checklist covers every automation opportunity I've found across dozens of agencies. Nine categories, 54 items. You don't need to do all of them — but if you're doing fewer than 10, you're leaving time and money on the table.

Print it out. Work through one section a week. By the end, your agency will run differently.

## 1. Setup

- Identify your top 3 time-consuming admin tasks and time them
- Create a shared digital folder for all care documentation (Google Drive or SharePoint)
- Set up a free AI assistant (Claude or ChatGPT) with your agency context
- Write a one-page brief: your agency size, services, CQC rating, and biggest pain points
- Give your AI tool your care plan template so it learns your format
- Bookmark the CQC regulatory guidance page for your service type

## 2. Compliance & CQC

- Auto-generate CQC evidence packs from existing care records
- Set up alerts for CQC policy changes that affect your registration
- Use AI to draft and update your Statement of Purpose annually
- Create a compliance calendar with automated reminders for all regulatory deadlines
- Generate mock inspection questions and practice responses monthly
- Build an audit trail template that auto-logs care activities

## 3. Care Plans

- Use AI to draft person-centred care plans from assessment notes
- Set review reminders — every care plan should be reviewed on schedule
- Auto-generate risk assessments from care plan data
- Create care plan update templates for common changes (medication, mobility, diet)
- Use AI to translate care plans into plain English for families
- Build a library of care plan phrases for consistency across your team

## 4. Staff Management

- Auto-generate supervision notes templates before each session
- Track training expiry dates with automated alerts (manual handling, safeguarding, first aid)
- Use AI to draft job adverts from your person spec
- Create onboarding checklists that auto-assign tasks to new starters
- Build a skills matrix to spot training gaps before CQC does
- Generate staff performance summaries from supervision records

## 5. Family Communication

- Draft monthly family update letters from care notes
- Create templated responses for common family enquiries
- Auto-generate incident report summaries in family-friendly language
- Set up a schedule for proactive family check-in calls with talking points
- Build FAQ documents for new admissions based on your most-asked questions
- Use AI to translate communications for families whose first language isn't English

## 6. Reporting & KPIs

- Build a live KPI dashboard (medication errors, falls, hospital admissions, complaints)
- Set up automated weekly reports for your registered manager
- Track occupancy rates and forecast trends monthly
- Create a CQC-ready quality assurance report template
- Monitor staff turnover rate and flag when it exceeds your benchmark
- Auto-generate commissioner reports from your existing data

## 7. Finance & Admin

- Automate invoice generation from care hours logged
- Set up alerts for overdue payments from commissioners or self-funders
- Use AI to reconcile timesheets against rotas weekly
- Create budget templates that auto-calculate staff costs by service type
- Draft funding applications using AI with your agency's data
- Track expenditure against budget with automated variance alerts

## 8. Time Savers

- Use AI to summarise long emails and extract action items
- Auto-transcribe team meetings and generate minutes
- Create email templates for recurring communications (spot checks, reviews, referrals)
- Set up automated calendar reminders for supervision cycles
- Use AI to draft policies from CQC guidance in your agency's voice
- Build quick-reference guides for common procedures

## 9. Growth

- Automate your enquiry response — acknowledge every new referral within 2 minutes
- Build an automated follow-up sequence for families who enquire but don't commit
- Track referral sources to see where your best business comes from
- Use AI to analyse competitor CQC reports and identify positioning opportunities
- Create automated review request emails for families after 90 days
- Set up a monthly newsletter template that pulls from your care activity data

## Where to Start

Don't try to do everything at once. Here's the order I recommend:

1. **Week 1:** Complete the Setup section — get your foundations in place
2. **Week 2:** Tackle Finance & Admin — this is where the money is
3. **Week 3:** Compliance & CQC — protect your rating
4. **Week 4:** Staff Management — reduce your admin burden on HR

The rest can follow at your own pace. But those first four weeks will change how your agency runs.
