## Why Your Data Is Hiding Money

Every small business I work with has the same problem. It's not a data problem. It's a visibility problem.

Your finances are in Xero or Sage. Your staff data is in BreatheHR — or a spreadsheet someone built in 2021 that nobody's touched since. Your operations are in something else entirely.

All of them running. All of them full of data. None of them talking to each other.

So what happens when you need to know how the business is doing? You open Xero. You check your inbox. You try to remember how busy the team was last month. Then you give up and go with your gut.

That's not a you problem. That's a systems problem.

And here's what it costs — not just time:

- Revenue is up but margin is down, and nobody can see it
- Three people off this week means delivery is about to drop, and nobody's connecting those dots
- £40,000 in receivables has been sitting at 90 days, hidden in Sage, with nobody flagging it

The money is there. The signal is there. But because nothing is connected, nobody's looking.

## The Discovery Process

Before I touch a tool with any client, I do a discovery. I map every system they're running. Every data source. Every number that actually drives the business.

Only then do I start building anything.

Here's why that matters: most businesses that come to me want a dashboard, or an automation, or "something with AI." But they don't know what they need connected. The discovery is what tells you where the value actually sits.

The process:

1. **Map every system** — what tools are running, what data lives where
2. **Identify every data source** — what's being captured, what's being missed
3. **Find the gaps** — which connections between systems would surface hidden value
4. **Prioritise** — which connections to build first, based on recoverable value

That's the process. And you can run the first two steps yourself, right now, for free.

## The Claude Discovery Prompt

Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). Paste this in. Fill in your own details.

```
I run a [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] in [LOCATION], UK.

Business Profile:
- [X] staff
- Approximately [X] clients
- [Regulated by: CQC / FCA / SRA / None]

Current Systems:
- [SYSTEM 1] for [PURPOSE]
  (e.g. SAGE for accounting: invoicing, payroll, and P&L reporting)
- [SYSTEM 2] for [PURPOSE]
  (e.g. BreatheHR for HR: staff records, absence, training dates)
- [SYSTEM 3] for [PURPOSE]
  (e.g. NOURISH for operations: care visits, client records, incidents)
- [SYSTEM 4] for [PURPOSE]
  (e.g. Google Sheets for reporting: manual weekly reports, KPI tracking)

Please analyse my business operations and produce:

1. A data discovery and dashboard strategy — what data exists in each
   system and what's being missed between them

2. A gap analysis — what connections between systems would surface
   hidden value (revenue leaks, compliance risks, operational blind spots)

3. A priority matrix — which connections to build first based on
   estimated recoverable value, with conservative annual estimates

For each finding, note:
- Which system(s) the data sits in
- Why it's not currently visible
- What connecting it would reveal
- A conservative annual value estimate where applicable
```

That's it. Three minutes. Fill in your systems, paste it in, and read what comes back.

## What You'll Get Back

Claude will return a structured analysis of your business. Here's what to look for:

**The data audit** breaks down what each of your systems captures and — more importantly — what falls between the cracks. Most businesses have 3-5 blind spots they've never noticed because each system only shows its own slice.

**The gap analysis** shows you which connections between systems would surface hidden value. This is where the money lives. Not in any single system, but in the space between them.

**The priority matrix** ranks the connections by estimated annual value. This is what tells you where to start.

When I ran this for Bluebell Care, three things jumped out immediately:

- Compliance risks buried in the care monitoring data — patterns that only show up when you look across a full month. Nobody was doing that.
- £60,000 in receivables sitting at 90 days. Hidden in Sage. Nobody flagging it.
- HR and operational data that had never been in the same room. They couldn't see the link between staffing levels and service quality.

None of that required a developer to spot. It just required someone to ask the right questions first.

## Three Routes to a Connected System

Now — getting from that discovery prompt to a live connected system. There are three honest routes.

### Route 1: Bring someone in

Here's why the discovery matters so much at this stage: you go into that conversation knowing exactly what you need. You're not paying a consultant to figure out your business. You're paying them to build something you've already mapped. That changes the brief completely.

### Route 2: n8n or Make

Automation tools. Visual. Drag and drop. Free to start. If you've got a few weekends and you're comfortable learning a new tool, this is how a lot of small businesses build their first connected workflow.

- [n8n.io](https://n8n.io) — open source, self-hosted or cloud
- [make.com](https://make.com) — cloud-based, visual builder

### Route 3: Claude Code

More powerful. Writes the scripts that pull data from your systems automatically. When your systems need to share data directly, it gets technical fast. But if you're curious, it's worth exploring.

The discovery work you've done — the mapping, the four questions, the prompt — that makes every path better, whichever one you choose.
