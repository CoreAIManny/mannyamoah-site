Every week, someone asks me: "Where do I start with AI?" Here are the five free tools I recommend to every client. No subscriptions needed. No credit card. Just tools that save real time from day one.

## 1. Claude (by Anthropic) — Your AI Business Brain

**What it does:** Drafts emails, summarises documents, writes policies, analyses data, builds spreadsheet formulas, generates reports.

**Why it's the best starting point:** Claude is the tool I use most. It handles nuance better than ChatGPT for business writing — policies, proposals, compliance docs. The free tier is generous enough for daily use.

**Try this tonight:**

> "I run a [type of business] with [X] employees. Our main admin tasks include [list 3-5 tasks]. Which of these could be automated with free tools, and what tools would you recommend for each one?"

That one prompt will give you a roadmap for your first automations.

**Cost:** Free (Pro is £18/month if you need more)

## 2. n8n — The Free Automation Engine

**What it does:** Connects your tools together. When something happens in one app, it triggers actions in others. Like Zapier, but free and self-hosted.

**Real example:** When a new enquiry comes in via email, n8n automatically creates a contact in your CRM, sends a confirmation email, and notifies your team on Slack. No human touches it.

**Why it matters:** Zapier charges £20+/month for basic automations. n8n does the same thing for free if you self-host, or £20/month for their cloud version with far more generous limits.

**Best for:** Connecting email, CRM, spreadsheets, Slack, and accounting tools.

**Cost:** Free (self-hosted) or £20/month (cloud)

## 3. Cal.com — Kill the "Does Tuesday Work?" Email Chain

**What it does:** People see your availability, pick a time, and it's booked. Automatic confirmation, automatic reminders, automatic calendar sync.

**Why you need it:** Every "does Tuesday work?" email chain wastes 15-20 minutes. Multiply that by 10 meetings a month and you're losing 3+ hours on scheduling alone.

**Set it up in 5 minutes:**
1. Go to cal.com and create a free account
2. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar
3. Set your available hours
4. Share the link in your email signature and social bio

**Cost:** Free for individuals

## 4. Tally — Forms That Don't Look Like 2005

**What it does:** Beautiful forms for enquiries, feedback, onboarding, and data collection. Integrates with everything. Unlimited forms and responses on the free plan.

**Why not Google Forms?** Tally looks professional, embeds cleanly on your website, and connects to tools like Notion, Slack, and email without Zapier.

**Use it for:**
- Client enquiry forms
- Staff feedback surveys
- Onboarding checklists
- Event registrations

**Cost:** Free (unlimited forms and responses)

## 5. Notion — Your Business Operating System

**What it does:** Notes, databases, project management, wikis, and documentation — all in one place. Think of it as a flexible, customisable workspace.

**Why it matters for small businesses:** Most SMBs have information scattered across emails, spreadsheets, documents, and someone's head. Notion puts it all in one searchable place.

**Start with:**
- A client database (name, status, last contact, next action)
- A task board for your team
- A folder for policies and procedures

**Cost:** Free for individuals, £8/month per user for teams

## The Stack in Action

Here's how these five tools work together:

1. **Claude** drafts your proposals, policies, and client communications
2. **Cal.com** handles all your meeting scheduling
3. **Tally** captures enquiries from your website
4. **n8n** connects everything — new Tally form → Notion database → email notification → calendar event
5. **Notion** is your central dashboard where everything lives

Total cost: £0/month.
